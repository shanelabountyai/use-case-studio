Discovery Intake

Fill this out to describe an AI use case you'd like evaluated. When you're done, hit Submit at the bottom and it goes straight to the practitioner — who'll run it through the studio and return a build / refine / park recommendation with a delivery plan.

1. Estimates are fine — just label them as estimates. A rough number you flag as a guess is more useful than a blank.

2. Try to land a measurable acceptance bar. A single number or threshold that would let everyone agree "this works."

00 Who's filling this out
01 Business understanding Problem · Current cost · Users · Outcome · Acceptance bar
Consider
  • What decision or outcome does this use case change, and who owns that outcome?
  • What does the current process cost today — hours, dollars, error rate — and how is that measured?
  • What happens if you do nothing for another year?
If you're not sure
  • Cost not quantified? Walk through a recent week — roughly how many hours, and how much rework?
  • No acceptance bar yet? What single number or threshold would let everyone agree this works?
02 Data Sources · Format · Volume · Sensitivity · Freshness
Consider
  • Where does the data live today, who owns access, and how is it granted?
  • How clean are the sources really — vs. how clean everyone assumes they are?
  • Are there examples of correct / incorrect outcomes that could become an evaluation set?
03 The task Task shape · Volume
Consider
  • In one sentence, what does the system actually do with each item?
04 Constraints Latency · Oversight · Budget · Compliance
Consider
  • What are the hard constraints — latency, budget, regulatory obligations?
  • What level of human oversight is required, and who staffs the review?
  • What existing systems must this fit into, and who controls them?
05 Success & adoption
Consider
  • Who has to change how they work for this to deliver value — and are they bought in?
  • How would you run a pilot, and what would make you comfortable expanding it?
  • What's been tried before here, and why did it stall?
