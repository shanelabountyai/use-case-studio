## Executive summary

Grant proposal first-draft assembly is a Quick win (composite 80) and proceeds to BUILD on the recommended pattern: direct prompting grounded with RAG over the approved reference corpus (boilerplate library, five years of submitted proposals with funded/declined outcomes, annual impact reports). The build is deliberately thin — a batch drafting job, not a chat product: a grant writer selects a funder brief and target sections, the system retrieves approved passages and prior funded language, and composes a sectioned first draft in which every factual claim and every number carries an inline citation back to its source document and location. The spine is the acceptance bar: grant writers rate ≥70% of drafts as a 'usable starting point' across 20 proposals, and zero unsourced statistics. The second half of that bar dictates the core engineering decision — numbers are not generated, they are retrieved and quoted. A numeric-claim verification step compares every figure in the draft against the retrieved evidence and flags or strips anything it cannot match, because fabricated outcome figures are a fraud exposure rather than a quality defect. Oversight is a launch condition: no draft leaves the review queue without a named grant writer signing off. Low task volume (18 proposals a year) and batch latency mean throughput engineering is irrelevant; corpus quality, citation integrity and reviewer trust are the whole game.

**Architecture pattern:** Direct prompting grounded with RAG over reference material

## Plan

### Architecture

**Pattern (from grounding, unchanged): Direct prompting grounded with RAG over reference material.**

Components:

1. **Approved-source corpus.** A curated document store holding only material cleared for funder representations: the boilerplate library, submitted proposals (tagged funded/declined), and annual impact reports. Anything not in this store cannot be cited, and anything not citable cannot appear as a fact in a draft. Curation is an explicit gate owned by the development lead, not an ingestion side effect.
2. **Ingestion + chunking pipeline.** Document conversion → section-aware chunking (proposals split by canonical section: mission, governance, past outcomes, programme description, budget narrative) → embedding → a vector index with metadata filters. Metadata carries at minimum: source type, document title, year, section label, funder, outcome (funded/declined/not applicable), and a page/paragraph anchor for citation.
3. **Funder brief intake.** The writer supplies the funder's stated priorities (pasted call text or a short structured form) plus the required section list and word limits. This is the query-shaping input; the system does not guess funder priorities.
4. **Retrieval layer.** Per target section, a filtered retrieval call: section-label filter plus priority-term query, with an optional preference for chunks from funded proposals. Retrieval returns candidate passages with anchors intact. Recency filters matter for outcome figures — impact numbers are pulled from the latest impact report unless the writer overrides.
5. **Composition step.** A single grounded prompt per section: the retrieved passages, the funder priority statement, the house-style instruction, and a hard instruction that every factual claim and every numeral must be followed by a citation token referencing a supplied chunk id. Sections are composed independently, then assembled, so a weak section can be regenerated without redrafting the whole proposal.
6. **Numeric-claim verifier (non-negotiable).** A deterministic post-step extracts every number, percentage, currency amount and date from the draft and attempts an exact or normalised match against the retrieved chunk text. Unmatched numbers are not silently kept: they are replaced with a visible placeholder marker and logged as a verification failure. This is the mechanism that carries the 'zero unsourced statistics' half of the acceptance bar; it is checkable and does not rely on model self-report.
7. **Citation resolver.** Citation tokens are resolved into human-readable references (document, section, page/paragraph) so a reviewer can jump to the source in seconds. Unresolvable tokens are treated as verification failures.
8. **Review queue (mandatory HITL).** Drafts land in a queue with the draft, the resolved citations, the verification report, and the placeholder markers surfaced first. A named grant writer accepts, edits, or rejects with a reason code. Nothing reaches a funder-facing document without that sign-off.
9. **Versioning and logging.** Prompts, chunking config, retrieval parameters and model configuration are versioned like code; every draft record stores the config version, retrieved chunk ids and verification outcome so a later question about a number is answerable.

Deliberately out of scope for the pilot: full-proposal autonomous submission, budget table generation, multi-turn editing chat, and any generation of programme-specific claims the programme leads have not already written down.

### Data Pipeline

Volume is small, sensitivity is internal, freshness is static — so the pipeline optimises for provenance fidelity, not scale.

**Inventory and access (first).** List every candidate file with owner, location and access path. Expect the stated data readiness score to move after first contact with the actual files: prior proposals typically exist as final PDFs and a scatter of near-final drafts, and distinguishing the submitted version from a working copy is a real task. Only submitted versions with confirmed outcomes enter the corpus.

**Curation gate.** Each document is classified as: approved-for-citation, reference-only (style/structure but not facts), or excluded (superseded figures, unapproved drafts, confidential funder correspondence). Superseded impact figures are the highest-risk category — an old outcome number that reads as current is exactly the failure the compliance note warns about. Excluded documents are removed from the index, not just down-ranked.

**Conversion and cleaning.** PDF/DOC → text with layout preserved enough to keep page and paragraph anchors. Strip funder-specific headers, footers, page numbers, and submission cover sheets. Preserve tables as text blocks with their captions — outcome figures usually live in tables, and losing the caption loses the meaning of the number.

**Chunking.** Section-aware rather than fixed-width: split on canonical proposal sections and headed subsections, keeping each chunk large enough to carry a complete claim with its qualifier ('served X families *in the 2023 programme year*'). A qualifier separated from its number is a provenance failure waiting to happen. Every chunk retains a stable id and a back-anchor.

**Metadata enrichment.** Section label, year, funder, funded/declined outcome, document type, approved-for-citation flag, and a figure-bearing flag for chunks containing numerals. The figure-bearing flag lets retrieval guarantee that outcome-heavy sections get evidence-carrying passages.

**Figure register.** A small structured table of the organisation's canonical statistics — the numbers that recur across proposals — each with its authoritative source, period and approval date. The verifier checks the register first; the register is the single place a stale number gets corrected.

**Refresh.** Static corpus, so refresh is event-driven: new impact report published, new proposal submitted with a known outcome, or boilerplate revised. The pipeline is scripted and repeatable so a refresh is one command plus a curation review, not a manual rebuild. Refresh triggers a rerun of the golden set before the updated index is promoted.

**Quality checks in the pipeline.** Chunk-anchor resolvability, orphaned-numeral detection (numbers whose qualifier was split away), duplicate-passage collapse across near-identical prior proposals, and a report of documents that failed conversion. Failures block promotion of the index.

### Evaluation

The acceptance bar has two independent halves and each needs its own measurement.

**Half one — usefulness: ≥70% of drafts rated 'usable starting point' across 20 proposals.**
- Build a written rubric with the grant writers before any drafting runs. 'Usable starting point' must be defined operationally — candidate anchors: correct sections present, house voice recognisable, funder priorities visibly addressed, and the writer estimates less rework than starting from a blank page. The rubric is the artefact that makes the 70% meaningful; without it the number is opinion.
- Golden set: assemble 50–100 scored items spanning routine funders, awkward ones (unusual section structures, tight word limits), and deliberately hard cases (a funder priority the organisation has never addressed, a call requiring figures the corpus does not contain). Include declined-proposal contexts so the system is not only rewarded for imitating past success.
- Scoring is human rubric scoring on the pilot sample. An LLM-as-judge may be introduced to scale rubric scoring, but only after judge-versus-human agreement is measured on a subsample and reported; judge agreement is an empirical result, not an assumption. If agreement is weak, human scoring stands.
- Rate drafts independently before the writer edits — post-edit satisfaction measures the writer, not the system.

**Half two — provenance: zero unsourced statistics.**
- This is a hard gate, not a percentage to trend. Measurement: for each draft, every extracted numeral is either matched to a retrieved chunk or the figure register, or it appears as a visible placeholder. A number present in prose without a resolvable citation is a bar failure for that draft and a defect for the build.
- Reviewers spot-check citations for *correctness*, not just presence: does the cited passage actually support the claim, and is the period right? Citation-present-but-wrong is the subtler failure and belongs in the red-team set.

**Failure taxonomy.** Log every failure as retrieval miss (evidence existed, was not retrieved), corpus gap (evidence did not exist), composition error (evidence retrieved, claim misstated), provenance error (number or citation unresolvable), or formatting/style. Each has a different fix and mixing them hides the real cause.

**Red-teaming.** Explicitly probe: a funder call asking for a statistic the corpus lacks (correct behaviour is a placeholder plus a flag, never a plausible invention); a call inviting superlative claims; two impact reports with conflicting figures for the same programme; a request that would pull a declined proposal's weakest framing. Findings become golden-set items.

**Staged rollout.** Shadow mode on live upcoming proposals — the system drafts, writers work as normal, outputs compared and rubric-scored — then limited release through the mandatory review queue on a subset of funders, then wider use only once the bar holds on real proposals. Pre-committed rollback trigger stated in Governance.

### Governance

**Oversight is a launch condition.** Every draft passes the review queue with a named grant writer sign-off before it informs any funder-facing document. There is no configuration in the pilot that bypasses this, and no 'trusted funder' fast path.

**Fraud-exposure controls.** The compliance note is the sharpest constraint in the case: a fabricated outcome figure is a fraud exposure. Controls, layered:
- Numbers are retrieved and quoted, never composed. The verifier enforces this mechanically.
- Unverifiable numbers become visible placeholders that a human must fill from an approved source — an obvious blank beats a plausible fiction.
- The figure register is the single authority for recurring statistics, with a named owner and a review date.
- Reviewers confirm citation *support*, not merely citation presence, on outcome and impact claims.
- Superseded figures are excluded from the index at curation, so a stale number cannot be retrieved in the first place.

**Roles.** Development lead owns the acceptance bar and the corpus curation gate. Grant writers are the accountable reviewers and the rubric authority. Programme leads own the substance of programme-specific claims and the figure register entries for their programmes — the system assembles, it does not originate programme facts. A delivery owner holds the rollback trigger.

**Traceability.** Each draft record stores: config and prompt version, index version, retrieved chunk ids, verification report, reviewer identity, decision and reason code. If a funder later queries a figure, the chain from claim to source document is reconstructable.

**Rollback trigger (pre-committed, before launch).** Any confirmed unsourced or unsupported statistic reaching a submitted proposal pauses the system pending root-cause analysis — this is a single-instance trigger, not a rate. Separately, if the rubric 'usable starting point' rate falls materially below the agreed bar over a rolling window of reviewed drafts, the system reverts to shadow mode. The exact window and the material-shortfall definition are set with the owner during Phase 0 and written down before first release.

**Data handling.** Internal sensitivity: corpus access is restricted to the development team and the grant writers. Funder correspondence and any personal data in beneficiary case studies are excluded or de-identified at curation. Data-handling terms for any external model or embedding service — retention, training use — are reviewed and recorded before the corpus is processed.

**Ongoing metric.** Reviewer agreement and reviewer edit-reason distribution are tracked as trust indicators. A rising share of provenance-related reason codes is treated as a corpus or verifier defect, not reviewer fussiness.

### Delivery

Small budget, one-quarter pilot, batch latency, 18 proposals a year. That shapes delivery: a small team, a thin build, and evaluation effort weighted toward corpus curation and rubric definition rather than infrastructure.

**Team shape (estimate):** one engineer able to build the ingestion pipeline and the drafting job, part-time involvement from the development lead for curation and the acceptance bar, and scheduled grant-writer time for rubric authoring and draft scoring. Reviewer time is the scarcest input — the pilot fails quietly if writer scoring hours are not booked in advance.

**Sequencing rationale.** Corpus curation and the rubric come before any generation work, because both halves of the acceptance bar are unmeasurable without them. The numeric verifier is built alongside the first drafting loop, not bolted on later — retrofitting provenance into a working generator is the common way this class of project misses its bar.

**Build the thinnest version first.** One funder brief, three sections, end-to-end with citations and verification, scored against the rubric. Resist adding sections, editing UI or funder-specific tuning until that core loop has a measured score.

**Capabilities, not products.** A managed or self-hosted vector store with metadata filtering; a general-purpose instruction-following language model reachable via API with acceptable data-handling terms; a lightweight review interface (a queue view over the draft, citations and verification report — a simple internal tool is sufficient at this volume); scripted, version-controlled ingestion. Selection is the team's call against cost and data-handling constraints.

**Cost posture.** Low task volume means inference cost is a minor line item; the dominant costs are engineering time and reviewer hours. Budget monitoring should track human hours, not tokens.

**Definition of done for the pilot.** Rubric scores collected on 20 proposals, provenance gate holding, rollback trigger documented and exercised at least once in a drill, refresh pipeline runnable by someone other than its author, and a written decision on whether to continue, extend or stop.

### Integration Notes

**Batch, not interactive.** The drafting job runs on request against a funder brief and returns a document; there is no need for streaming responses or low-latency serving. This removes an entire class of infrastructure work and should be stated explicitly so it is not reintroduced by habit.

**Output format.** Deliver in the format grant writers already edit in — a word-processing document with citations as visible inline references or comments, and placeholders styled to be impossible to miss. If citations are stripped by an export step, the provenance guarantee is lost in transit; test the export path, not just the generator.

**Where it sits in the existing workflow.** The system replaces the blank-page start, not the writer's judgement. Handover point is the review queue; downstream, the writer's edited draft becomes the working document and — once submitted with a known outcome — a future corpus candidate. That feedback loop should be deliberate: submitted-and-outcome-known documents re-enter via the curation gate, never automatically.

**Interfaces to keep thin.** Funder brief intake can start as a form or pasted text; resist building a funder-priority taxonomy until the pilot shows retrieval genuinely needs one.

**Corpus refresh coordination.** Impact report publication is the highest-impact refresh event, since it supersedes figures. Tie the refresh to that publication calendar and rerun the golden set before promoting the updated index.

## Data flows

**Corpus ingestion and curation:** Inventory candidate documents with owner and access path; confirm which prior proposals are the submitted versions → Curation gate: classify each document as approved-for-citation, reference-only, or excluded (superseded figures, unapproved drafts, confidential correspondence) → Convert to text preserving page/paragraph anchors and table captions → Section-aware chunking; keep numbers with their qualifying period and scope in the same chunk → Enrich metadata: section label, year, funder, funded/declined, document type, approved-for-citation, figure-bearing flag → Populate the figure register with canonical statistics, authoritative source, period and approval date → Embed and load into the vector index with metadata filters → Run pipeline quality checks (anchor resolvability, orphaned numerals, duplicate collapse, conversion failures); block promotion on failure
**Draft assembly run:** Grant writer submits funder brief, required section list and word limits → Per section: filtered retrieval over approved-for-citation chunks using section label plus funder priority terms, with figure-bearing chunks required for outcome sections → Grounded composition prompt per section with retrieved passages, funder priorities, house-style instruction and mandatory citation tokens → Assemble sections into a single draft → Numeric-claim verifier extracts every numeral and matches it against retrieved chunk text or the figure register → Unmatched numbers replaced with visible placeholders and logged as verification failures → Citation resolver turns tokens into readable references; unresolvable tokens logged as failures → Emit draft plus resolved citations plus verification report to the review queue → Log config version, index version, retrieved chunk ids and verification outcome
**Human review and sign-off:** Reviewer opens the queue item with verification report and placeholders surfaced first → Reviewer checks that each cited passage actually supports its claim, including the period and scope of each figure → Reviewer fills placeholders from an approved source or removes the claim → Reviewer records accept / edit / reject with a reason code → Named sign-off recorded before the draft is used in any funder-facing document → Reason codes and reviewer agreement aggregated as ongoing trust metrics → Provenance-related failures routed back as corpus or verifier defects and added to the golden set
**Evaluation loop:** Grant writers author the rubric operationalising 'usable starting point' → Assemble a 50–100 item golden set spanning routine, awkward and deliberately hard cases, including corpus-gap cases → Run candidate configurations against the golden set; score usefulness by human rubric application → Apply the provenance gate as pass/fail per draft: every numeral resolvable or visibly placeheld → Classify every failure as retrieval miss, corpus gap, composition error, provenance error, or formatting → If LLM-as-judge is introduced, measure and report judge-versus-human agreement on a subsample before relying on it → Feed red-team findings and live review defects back into the golden set → Rerun the golden set before promoting any prompt, config or index change

## Assumptions & labeled estimates

- All durations and phase lengths are estimates for planning only, not commitments; they assume a single engineer with part-time development-lead and grant-writer involvement.
- The team-shape sizing (one engineer plus part-time lead and scheduled writer hours) is an estimate, not a costed staffing plan.
- Grant-writer hours for rubric authoring and independent draft scoring can be booked in advance; the evaluation design depends on this and degrades without it.
- Prior submitted proposals can be reliably distinguished from working drafts, and funded/declined outcomes are recorded or recoverable.
- Documents convert to text with page or paragraph anchors preserved well enough to support citations; heavily scanned or image-only files may need separate handling.
- The canonical recurring statistics can be agreed and owned in a figure register by programme leads within Phase 0.
- Data-handling terms (retention, training use) for any external model or embedding service can be reviewed and accepted before the corpus is processed.
- Data readiness may score lower after hands-on inspection of the actual files than the initial assessment suggests; Phase 1 exit criteria are the checkpoint for that.
- Reaching 20 scored proposals within a single pilot quarter assumes the annual proposal cadence supplies enough live cases; otherwise the count is completed with retrospective golden-set cases and this is recorded as such.

## Independent critic audit

**Critic verdict:** SHIP WITH FIXES

**Load-bearing gaps**
- **Numeric verifier false-positive rate is never measured** — The design leans entirely on a deterministic matcher: "A deterministic post-step extracts every number ... and attempts an exact or normalised match against the retrieved chunk text." String-matching a numeral to a chunk proves the digits exist somewhere in the evidence, not that the number means what the draft says (right programme, right year, right denominator). The plan pushes this to reviewers ("Reviewers spot-check citations for *correctness*") but defines no metric, sample rate, or pass threshold for matched-but-wrong figures. Add a measured citation-support accuracy check on a fixed sample per phase, with its own pass/fail, or the 'zero unsourced statistics' gate is only a digit-presence gate.
- **No degraded-mode or failure path for the run itself** — Nothing says what happens when retrieval returns nothing usable for a required section, the model API is unavailable mid-run, or the verifier itself errors. The only defined failure output is a placeholder for an unmatched number. Define: partial-draft delivery rules, a hard abort that emits no draft rather than an unverified one, and whether a verifier crash blocks queue emission.
- **Sample-size honesty for the ≥70% measurement** — The bar is 'across 20 proposals' but the pilot quarter plausibly supplies far fewer live cases. The plan needs, before build, an explicit statement of what a ≥70% reading on 5 live proposals does and does not license, and whether retrospective golden-set cases count toward the bar or only toward diagnostics. Right now Assumption 9 lets the count be quietly padded.
- **Conflicting-figure resolution has no owner in the flow** — Red-teaming probes "two impact reports with conflicting figures for the same programme", and curation excludes superseded figures, but no data flow or role names who adjudicates a live conflict discovered after promotion, or how the figure register is corrected mid-pilot with an audit trail.

**Top fixes**
- Rewrite the Phase 5 exit criterion to state the achievable live-proposal count for one quarter and, explicitly, how the remainder of the 20 is composed (retrospective golden-set cases) and what that does to the strength of the ≥70% claim — do not leave the reconciliation buried in Assumption 9.
- Add a measured citation-support check with its own threshold and sample size, and soften "The verifier enforces this mechanically" / "cannot be retrieved in the first place" / "provenance guarantee" to what a numeral matcher plus human review actually delivers.
- Reconcile the two statements of the provenance half — per-draft gate (Evaluation) vs 'reaching a submitted proposal' (Governance/Phase 5) — to one definition, and either fix the rollback window/material-shortfall numbers now or remove "rollback trigger thresholds written down" from the Phase 0 exit criterion.

**Overclaims flagged**
- "Numbers are retrieved and quoted, never composed. The verifier enforces this mechanically." — a normalised string match cannot enforce that a number was not composed; it can only detect numerals absent from the retrieved text. Composed-but-coincidentally-matching figures pass.
- "Superseded figures are excluded from the index at curation, so a stale number cannot be retrieved in the first place." — this holds only for figures identified as superseded; it promises impossibility from a manual curation judgement.
- "If a funder later queries a figure, the chain from claim to source document is reconstructable." — reconstructable only if anchors resolved correctly and the index version is retained; the plan itself flags unresolvable anchors as a known failure mode.
- "If citations are stripped by an export step, the provenance guarantee is lost in transit" — the word 'guarantee' overstates what a flag-and-placeholder pipeline with human review delivers; it is a control, not a guarantee.

**Fabrication scan**
- `prior proposals typically exist as final PDFs and a scatter of near-final drafts` → must-label
- `retrofitting provenance into a working generator is the common way this class of project misses its bar` → must-label
- `Low task volume (18 proposals a year) and batch latency mean throughput engineering is irrelevant` → ok
- `A managed or self-hosted vector store with metadata filtering; a general-purpose instruction-following language model reachable via API ... Selection is the team's call` → ok

**Consistency issues**
- Phase 5 exit criterion demands "Across 20 proposals, ≥70% rated 'usable starting point'" inside the "Remainder of the pilot quarter", but the grounding states 18 proposals a year — roughly 4–5 live proposals per quarter. Assumption 9 admits the shortfall and offers retrospective golden-set substitution, but the milestone exit criterion never references that fallback, so the plan's own spine milestone is arithmetically unreachable as written.
- Governance narrows the bar: the rollback trigger and Phase 5 exit speak of "zero unsourced statistics reaching a submitted proposal", while the grounding bar and the Evaluation section treat it as a per-draft gate ("A number present in prose without a resolvable citation is a bar failure for that draft"). Two different thresholds for the same half of the bar.
- Phase 4 exit criterion "usefulness rate trending at or above the agreed bar" is unmeasurable — no sample size, no threshold, and the rubric-scored shadow sample size is never stated, unlike Phase 3's 50–100 items.
- Governance defers the rollback numbers: "The exact window and the material-shortfall definition are set with the owner during Phase 0", yet Phase 0's exit criterion claims "rollback trigger thresholds written down" — the plan both defers and claims completion of the same artefact without saying who arbitrates the number.
- Integration Notes route submitted drafts back into the corpus ("submitted-and-outcome-known documents re-enter via the curation gate"), but the Evaluation section never tests whether re-ingested model-assisted text creates self-citation loops (a draft's own earlier phrasing becoming its own evidence).

**Acceptance bar is the spine:** yes — Both halves drive structure: Phase 0 locks the rubric and figure register, Phase 2's exit is "every numeral ... matched to a source or rendered as a visible placeholder", Phase 3 gates offline on the provenance rule, and Phase 5 restates "≥70% rated 'usable starting point' ... and zero unsourced statistics".
**Verdict integrity:** pass — Plan verdict BUILD matches grounding verdict BUILD, keeps the recommended pattern unchanged, and does not inflate the composite or soften the oversight requirement. The only lean toward rosiness is the narrowed 'reaching a submitted proposal' phrasing of the provenance half, noted under consistency rather than drift.
